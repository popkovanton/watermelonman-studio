Car Pixels: Color by Number · Watermelon Man Studio

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Car Pixels: Color by Number (“Car Pixels”) or contact Watermelon Man Studio about the app.

Effective date / Last updated
October 5, 2026

Summary

Car Pixels is an Android car coloring game provided by Watermelon Man Studio, also known as “Авто: раскраска по номерам”. Its Android application ID is com.watermelonman.colorbynumber.cars. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and does not require registration or a game account.

Coloring progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your saved coloring progress. Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings. Google services handle analytics, crash reporting, advertising, privacy consent, and in-app purchases as described below.

Game data on your device

Car Pixels stores your coloring progress, the action history used to create coloring videos, remaining tools, unlocked pictures, and whether full access has been confirmed. It also stores settings, information about new pictures, and random reward identifiers to prevent the same advertising reward from being granted more than once.

This information lets you resume coloring, keep your settings, use unlocked content, and create images or videos. Game pictures are included in the app. Game progress is not sent to a Watermelon Man Studio server. The app does not request your date of birth or age category.

Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled for Car Pixels. Depending on your Android version and system settings, the operating system may include the app's database and settings in cloud backups or transfers to another device. These copies are managed by the operating system and its backup service. Temporary cache files are excluded from ordinary Android Auto Backup.

You can manage available backup and restore options in your device settings. See Android's Auto Backup information for details.

Advertising and privacy choices

Car Pixels uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for rewarded advertising. You may choose to watch an ad to unlock a picture or replenish tools. Ads may be loaded in advance during gameplay, before you select the option to watch one.

Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and available device or account identifiers. This information is used to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. The data processed depends on the service configuration, your privacy choices, device settings, and applicable requirements.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. More information is available in Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google User Messaging Platform updates consent information before ad requests and shows a consent form when required. Where available, you can revisit advertising privacy choices through the app's settings. The form identifies the advertising partners and choices applicable to you. Ad availability and personalization depend on those choices and the relevant service settings.

Purchasing full access removes offers to watch rewarded ads. Google services for purchase checks, privacy consent, analytics, and crash reporting may still operate after the purchase.

Purchases

Full access is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. The app obtains product and price information, checks purchase status, and acknowledges purchases through Google Play. Purchase tokens are used by the billing service when acknowledging a purchase. The app's local database stores whether full access is confirmed.

Google Play processes payments and payment details; we do not receive your bank or card details through Car Pixels. Purchase restoration is associated with your Google Play account. Clearing local game data does not cancel a purchase or delete Google Play's purchase history. Google's handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Images, videos, sharing, and reviews

Car Pixels creates PNG or GIF images and MP4 videos on your device and places them in temporary app storage. When you choose to share a file, Android gives the app or service you select access to that specific file. The recipient handles it according to its own privacy practices. The app-sharing option in settings shares only the game name and a Google Play link.

The option to rate the app opens Google Play, where Google handles any rating or review you submit.

Analytics

Car Pixels uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help Watermelon Man Studio understand how the app is used and improve its features. Google processes usage events, session statistics, app and device information, and an app-instance identifier that distinguishes installations of the app. Approximate location and available advertising identifiers may also be processed, depending on the app's configuration, device settings, and privacy choices.

Analytics information is sent to Google and made available to Watermelon Man Studio through analytics reports. These usage records are separate from the saved coloring progress and video files stored on your device. More information is available in Google's Analytics data collection information.

Crash reporting

Car Pixels uses Firebase Crashlytics to help Watermelon Man Studio diagnose errors and improve reliability. Reports sent to Google may contain crash and application-not-responding information, stack traces, error details, app and operating-system versions, device information, and installation or session identifiers. When integrated with Analytics, reports may also include a history of app events leading up to an error.

Watermelon Man Studio can view these diagnostic reports through Firebase. See Firebase's privacy and security information for details about the service's data handling.

Permissions

Car Pixels does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or broad access to device storage. Network access supports Google's analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, consent, and purchase services.

Google's advertising SDK may process its own diagnostic and interaction information as described above.

Support messages

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Please send only information necessary for the request.

We keep support messages and their attachments only for as long as needed to respond to and resolve your request, then delete them.

Third-party services

Google provides Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, and related Google Play services. Advertising partners identified in the applicable consent form may also process advertising information. Android's backup service and any app you choose for sharing may receive data as described above. Our support email is hosted by Gmail.

These providers may process information in countries other than where you live and handle information under their own privacy practices. You can find more information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites
  • Google advertising information
  • Google advertising consent information
  • Firebase privacy and security information

Storage and deletion

Local game data remains in the app's storage until you reset the relevant picture, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Resetting a picture deletes that picture's coloring progress and video history. Clearing app data removes local game data and settings. Android may clear temporary cache files automatically.

Local deletion does not cancel your Google Play purchase or automatically delete analytics, crash reports, or other information already sent to Google. System backups and files already shared with another app are managed separately; use the relevant system or service controls to manage or delete them. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved game data.

Analytics retention depends on the Google Analytics property settings and Google's policies. According to Firebase's published privacy information, Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting their removal from live and backup systems.

Google determines retention for other information processed by its advertising, consent, payment, and backup services under its policies and service settings. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Account data and privacy controls.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can reset a picture, clear Car Pixels' data, uninstall the app, manage Android backup and privacy controls, and revisit advertising privacy choices where offered in the app. There is no separate Car Pixels account to delete.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.

Contact us using the address below for requests about information we hold, including support messages. We do not receive your local game progress and may not be able to identify data held by Google or another service for a particular user. You can also use those services' own privacy and deletion controls.

Children

Car Pixels is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. This intended audience is separate from any store content rating. The app does not collect dates of birth or ask for an age category.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and delete information we hold where appropriate.

Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, and file sharing grants access to the file you select through Android's sharing mechanism. Third-party services apply their own security measures. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this address with an updated date.
